# User Guide: Movie Ratings and Reviews

Welcome to the Movie Rating System! This guide will help you understand how to use the newly implemented rating and review features.

## 1. Finding Content to Rate
- Browse the **Home Page** to see featured and trending movies.
- Use the **Discover** page (Search) to find specific titles by name, genre, or release year.
- Click on any movie or TV show card to go to its **Detail Page**.

## 2. Submitting a Rating
On the movie detail page, you will find the **Rate & Review** section:
1. **Choose a Score**: Hover over the stars and click to select a rating from **1 to 10**.
   - 1-3: Poor
   - 4-6: Average
   - 7-8: Good
   - 9-10: Excellent
2. **Add a Review (Optional)**: Type your thoughts in the text area. Tell the community what you liked or disliked about the movie.
3. **Submit**: Click the **"Submit Review"** button.
   - *Note: You must be logged in to submit a rating.*

## 3. Viewing Community Feedback
- Scroll down to the **User Reviews** section on the detail page.
- You can see ratings and comments from other users, including:
  - The user's name and avatar.
  - Their numeric score (out of 10).
  - The date they posted the review.
  - Their detailed commentary.

## 4. Managing Your Ratings
- Go to your **Profile Page** to see a summary of your activity.
- Under **Recent Activity**, you can find all the movies you've rated.
- Your **Statistics** page will show your average rating given and how your ratings are distributed across different genres.

## 5. Troubleshooting
- **Cannot see the rating stars?** Make sure you are logged in.
- **Review not submitting?** Ensure you have selected a numeric score before clicking submit.
- **Rating didn't update?** Try refreshing the page; however, the UI is designed to update instantly!

*Happy Watching and Rating!*
